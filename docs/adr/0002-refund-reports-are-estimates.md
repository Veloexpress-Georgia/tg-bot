# Refund reports are cumulative estimates, not payouts

A cancellation report tells Misho the total to return for the service day based
on received payments and remaining bookings. It includes amounts shown in earlier
reports; it is not an additional refund. Misho may settle later, outside the bot.

Creating or repeating a report must not append a refund to the payment ledger.
Actual payouts are not tracked. Keep the rendered estimate for admin reference,
with received money, money retained for rides, and a short cumulative-total note.
Cancelling the whole day still retires the live payment projection to keep a new
poll empty, but does not erase or reverse received-payment history.
